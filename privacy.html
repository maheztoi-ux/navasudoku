Privacy Policy for Neo Sudoku: Classic & Color

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Neo Sudoku: Classic & Color ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neo Sudoku is built as an offline puzzle game. We do not require user account creation, registration, or login. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or IP address.

2. Local Device Data Storage

All gameplay information is strictly stored locally on your device. This data includes:

  • Current puzzle save states, hints, and active grid board notes
  • In-app display preferences (such as Light or Dark theme settings)
  • Gameplay options (such as active timers and mistake limit counters)
  • Performance records, completion star ratings, and game progress

This information never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by us.

3. Third-Party Services

The core gameplay functions completely offline without sending data to external servers. The App does not integrate third-party tracking, profiling analytics, or targeted advertising frameworks that collect personal data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, Neo Sudoku is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is safe for players of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
